第三站失腳
Stop 3 · Stumbled
（太 26:33至35；參太 26:69至75）

彼得說：眾人雖然為你的緣故跌倒，我卻永不跌倒。

耶穌說：我實在告訴你，今夜雞叫以先，你要三次不認我。

彼得說：我就是必須和你同死，也總不能不認你。眾門徒都是這樣說。

（正當耶穌被審訊的時候）

彼得在外面院子裡坐著，有一個使女前來，說：你素來也是同那加利利人耶穌一夥的。彼得在眾人面前卻不承認，說：我不知道你說的是什麼！既出去，到了門口，又有一個使女看見他，就對那裡的人說：這個人也是同拿撒勒人耶穌一夥的。彼得又不承認，並且起誓說：我不認得那個人。過了不多的時候，旁邊站著的人前來，對彼得說：你真是他們一黨的，你的口音把你露出來了。彼得就發咒起誓的說：我不認得那個人。立時，雞就叫了。

彼得想起耶穌所說的話：雞叫以先，你要三次不認我。

反思

火熱的彼得口口聲聲地在主耶穌面前說自己不會跌倒，然而，當耶穌受審的時候，卻三次不認主。

如果你是彼得，你認為你會有這份信心嗎？

KJV · Matt 26:33 to 35; cf. Matt 26:69 to 75

Peter answered and said unto him, Though all men shall be offended because of thee, yet will I never be offended.

Jesus said unto him, Verily I say unto thee, That this night, before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice.

Peter said unto him, Though I should die with thee, yet will I not deny thee. Likewise also said all the disciples.

(While Jesus was on trial)

Now Peter sat without in the palace: and a damsel came unto him, saying, Thou also wast with Jesus of Galilee. But he denied before them all, saying, I know not what thou sayest. And when he was gone out into the porch, another maid saw him, and said unto them that were there, This fellow was also with Jesus of Nazareth. And again he denied with an oath, I do not know the man. And after a while came unto him they that stood by, and said to Peter, Surely thou also art one of them; for thy speech bewrayeth thee. Then began he to curse and to swear, saying, I know not the man. And immediately the cock crew.

And Peter remembered the word of Jesus, which said unto him, Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice.

Reflection

Fervent Peter had boldly told Jesus he would never fall away. Yet when Jesus was put on trial, he denied his Lord three times.

If you were Peter, do you think you would have had that kind of faith?
